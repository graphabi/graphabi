# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful feedback, responsibility, and focus on what is best
for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language or attention,
insults, trolling, threats, publishing others' private information, or conduct inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take fair
corrective action in response to behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or
harmful. They may remove or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions, and will
communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies in community spaces and when an individual officially represents the community.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately using the security contact
documented in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. Community leaders will
respect the reporter's privacy and safety.

## Enforcement guidelines

Leaders will consider context and impact when choosing a response: correction for inappropriate
language, warning for a single incident, temporary restriction for sustained behavior, or permanent
removal for repeated harassment or threats.
